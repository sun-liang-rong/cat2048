# Kitchen game art direction

## Style lock

- Warm, healing, lightly stylized 3D mobile-game art.
- Fixed frontal view with a slight top-down angle.
- Soft key light from the upper left; shadows fall toward the lower right.
- Cream kitchen palette with colorful, moderately saturated objects.
- Rounded, clean silhouettes and tactile materials.
- No outlines that are too heavy, photorealism, text, brands, watermarks, QR codes, people, or hands unless explicitly requested.

## Scene composition

- Design at a 750 × 1334 portrait target.
- Keep the center and middle-lower portion readable as the play field.
- Reserve the bottom interface region for the six-slot tray; do not paint the tray into the kitchen background.
- Include an empty fruit basket, an empty cup rack, and an empty bread basket as non-interactive storage destinations.
- Do not paint red apples, cream mugs, toast slices, or lookalike loose items into the background.
- Avoid excessive small props, busy wallpaper, illegible labels, and strong depth-of-field blur.

## Item tiles

- Render exactly one object, centered on a transparent canvas.
- Use a square source composition with the object occupying 75%–85% of the canvas.
- Preserve a small soft contact shadow with clean semitransparent edges.
- Keep the complete object inside the canvas with comfortable padding.
- Ensure each object remains recognizable at approximately 80 px.
- Avoid plates, packaging, labels, additional ingredients, decorative text, or multiple copies.

## UI art

- Use cream ceramic or pale warm wood with subtle depth.
- Keep slot interiors empty, even, and high contrast enough for object tiles.
- Tutorial hand should be a friendly simplified pointer with one clear fingertip and no sleeve text or jewelry.

## Level 001 visual roles

| Asset | Shape role | Color role | Storage destination |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Red apple | Round | Strong warm red | Fruit basket |
| Cream mug | Round body with handle | Pale cream / soft blue accent | Cup rack |
| Toast | Rounded square | Golden yellow and light brown | Bread basket |

These three silhouettes must be instantly distinguishable without relying only on color.
